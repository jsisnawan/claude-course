    // THREAT_ASSESSMENT

    Travel smart, travel safe.

    General advisories — always cross-check with your government's travel notices before you fly.

    Travel insurance

    Always buy comprehensive cover. Medical evacuation in Asia can run into five figures SGD.

    Embassy contacts

    Save your embassy's address and emergency line offline. Screenshot it before you fly.

    Scam awareness

    Use metered taxis, avoid "closed temple" tuk-tuk stories, never hand over your passport at small hotels.

    Water & food

    Bottled or filtered water in most of SE Asia. Eat where locals queue — busy stalls turn over fresh stock.

    Transport

    Prefer Grab, Uber, or licensed taxis. Helmet on every motorbike ride. Trust your gut at night.

    Health

    Pack a basic kit: rehydration salts, antihistamines, paracetamol, plasters. Check vaccine requirements 6 weeks out.
